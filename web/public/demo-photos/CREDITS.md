# Demo photos: where they come from

These 12 photos (plus one extra chassis label) are a **demo set**, not a real customer inspection.
They were assembled from free-licence photos on Wikimedia Commons and **edited** for the demo:

- the number plate **KA 01 AB 1234** on FRONT, FRONT_LEFT_45, REAR, REAR_LEFT_45 and REAR_RIGHT_45 is **synthetic**,
  pasted over a plate the original photographer had already blurred;
- on REAR the synthetic plate fully covers the original photo's real plate, and the rear window is blurred;
- the plate on FRONT_RIGHT_45 was blurred by us;
- the parking-garage photos and the odometer were brightened a little;
- FRONT, REAR, LEFT_SIDE and RIGHT_SIDE are crops; REAR_RIGHT_45 is a left-right mirror of the rear-left photo
  (so the "NEXON" badge reads backwards);
- the chassis labels (CHASSIS_NUMBER*.jpg) are **made up** and drawn by us. They are not real chassis numbers;
- the photos do **not** all show the same physical car (three different blue Nexons, plus a Hyundai odometer and a
  Tata Indica EV engine bay).

They prove the app's flow and rules, not its real-world accuracy.
